Privacy Policy – Home Workout

AshStudio · Effective date: 28 October 2026

Summary

Home Workout keeps everything you enter on your own phone. We have no servers, no accounts and no analytics, and the app cannot connect to the internet, so your data is never sent to us or anyone else.

This policy explains what Home Workout ("the app"), published by AshStudio ("we", "us"), stores, why, and how you stay in control of it. It applies to the Android app distributed through Google Play.

Information the app stores

The app only stores what you type or tap into it. Some of it is health and fitness information, which we treat as sensitive.

WhatExamplesHealth or fitness data?
Quiz answersGender (optional), goal, focus areas, motivation, workout place and frequency, fitness level, stamina and flexibility answers, chosen rewardSome (fitness level, stamina, flexibility)
Body measurementsHeight, current weight, target weight, birth yearYes
Injuries you markShoulder, back, waist/hips, wrist, kneeYes

App settingsRest time, countdown length, voice guide and sound on/off, warm-up on/off, unitsNo

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, advertising ID or payment details. You never need an account.

How the app uses it

Everything is used on your phone, only to run the app for you:

  • Build your plan: your goal picks the plan; your fitness level and stamina set how long and how many reps; your injuries swap out exercises that load that body part.
  • Show estimates: your height and weight give your BMI, your estimated calories per workout, and a projected date for your target weight.
  • Track progress: your history shows completed days, plan progress and your streak.
  • Remember your preferences: settings and units stay as you left them.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, selling, or training any model, and we never see it.

Where it is stored and who sees it

Your information is stored only in the app's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. We do not share, sell or transfer it to anyone, because it never leaves your phone.

  • Voice guide: spoken cues are created by the text-to-speech engine already on your phone (usually Google's). The app passes it short phrases such as "Rest. Next up: squats, 12 reps", never your personal details. That engine is run by its own provider under their privacy policy.
  • Device backup: if you have turned on Android's own backup, Android may include the app's data in your Google account backup, under Google's policy. You control this in your phone's settings.
  • Google Play: Google may collect data about installs and crashes through Play, under Google's privacy policy. We do not add any extra tracking.

Permissions

The app asks for no permissions that need your approval. It declares two basic ones that Android grants automatically:

PermissionWhy
Keep the screen awake (WAKE_LOCK)Stops the screen turning off while you follow a workout
View network state (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE)Used by the built-in media player library. The app has no internet permission and sends nothing over the network

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it. Because we hold no copy, we cannot delete or recover it for you, and nothing remains with us after you delete it.

  1. Change answers: in the app, open Me → Retake the quiz.
  2. Delete everything: Android Settings → Apps → Home Workout → Storage → Clear storage.
  3. Remove the app: uninstalling deletes all of its data from the phone.

If Android backup is on, also delete the app's backup from your Google account settings.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, the EU/UK GDPR and California's CCPA give you rights to access, correct, delete and port your personal data, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a regulator.

Since all of your data is on your device, you exercise these rights directly: you can see and change it in the app and delete it at any time as described above. We never sell or share personal data. For any privacy question or complaint, email ashstudio.app@gmail.com and we will reply within 30 days.

Children, security and health

  • Children: the app is meant for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed at children or teenagers. The birth-year picker only allows ages 18 and up. If we learn that someone under 18 is using it, we will ask them to stop.
  • Security: your data sits in app-private storage protected by Android's app sandbox and your phone's screen lock and encryption. Keep your phone locked to protect it.
  • Not medical advice: plans, BMI, calorie and weight-date figures are general estimates, not medical advice. Check with a doctor before starting if you have an injury, a health condition or are pregnant, and stop if anything hurts.
